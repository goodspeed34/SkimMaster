Receiver pool: Each SDR front-end contributes one wideband IQ stream. Slices are allocated from these streams by the decoder scheduler.

Buffer fill: Percentage of the driver ring buffer currently occupied. Sustained values above 80% usually indicate USB or CPU starvation.

Drops: The number of IQ blocks discarded because the decoder pipeline could not keep up. Drops corrupt active decodes, so zero is the target.

Remote receivers: Network sources remain in Standby until a band schedule or manual allocation starts them.

Driver: Must match the physical device. The ALSA zero-IF option expects a stereo 48/96/192 kHz capture card; the I channel carries the real RF spectrum.

Center IF: The RF frequency at the middle of the IQ passband. Slices are referenced to this center, so changing it shifts every decoder assigned to the receiver.

Sample Rate: Determines maximum usable bandwidth. A 192 kHz rate cannot host a 912 kHz slice plan.

Swap I/Q: Corrects inverted spectrum on sound-card and direct-conversion front-ends.

Bias Tee: Supplies DC on the antenna port for powered preselectors. Never enable with a directly connected grounded antenna.

Baud / Shift: 45.45 Bd with 170 Hz shift is the standard amateur RTTY mode. Contest and DX stations occasionally use 50 or 75 Bd.

Mark / Space: Audio tones used for the two RTTY states. Changing these without changing the receiver tuning will invert the decoded character set.

AFC: Tracks slow tuning drift. A wide range increases CPU load and the chance of locking onto an adjacent signal.

Unshift-on-space: Standard RTTY behavior that returns to letters after a space, correcting a common loss-of-sync failure.

Clock accuracy: FT8, FT4, and WSPR expect UTC within about one second. Check the NTP status on the System Summary page before troubleshooting missing decodes.

Decode depth: Deep decoding finds weaker signals at the cost of CPU time. At high spot rates, use Normal or Fast to preserve real-time decoding.

AP decoding: A-priori decoding resolves partial messages using the callsign database. Aggressive mode can produce occasional false positives.

Uploads: PSKReporter receives FT8/FT4 reports; WSPRnet receives WSPR reports. Disable uploads when operating offline or during receiver testing.

Metrics: DT is the decode time offset; values beyond ±2 s usually indicate a clock or audio-buffer problem.

Noise Floor: Measured as the median power in unoccupied FFT bins, excluding detected carriers. It reflects antenna, feedline, and local RFI conditions.

Graceful restart: Waits for current decode slots to finish, flushes upload queues, then restarts. Adds up to one WSPR cycle of delay.
